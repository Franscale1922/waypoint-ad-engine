# Batch 4 — audience signal, competitor average, human-made animation (2026-09-27)

_Three vidIQ-led research passes (875 credits total). Built under the anti-average rule: findings are
sorted into THE AVERAGE (refuse), THE SIGNAL (the truth underneath), THE UNCLAIMED GROUND (where
recommendations may come from). Nothing here says "do what performs."_

## 1. The positioning this points to
**Every franchise voice talks about brands. Nobody talks about the buyer.** Competitors make
"$X to open a Chick-fil-A", brand-vs-brand lists, "what's the catch", success-rate stats; the
"uncomfortable truths about franchising" lane is already owned by the biggest channel (Franchise
Inspectors, 181K) and by finance creators ("Why I'd Never Open a Franchise"). The corporate
refugee's inner life — the lost title, the spouse, the 401(k) as lifeboat, being 54 and the new guy
— is unclaimed (the one channel aimed at 50+ buyers has 205 subscribers).
**Waypoint's lane: the buyer's truth, not the brand's economics.**

## 2. THE AVERAGE — banned list (checkable)
**Franchise content:** "You can open a [brand] for just $X" · "[Brand] vs [Brand]: which makes
more" · "X franchises that fail" · "what's the catch / what nobody tells you / the dark side" ·
top/best/most-profitable lists · success-rate percentages · "no money down" · "$X profit with 10
hours a week" / semi-absentee / passive income · fast-food brand bait (they rarely sell these) ·
talking head in office/car, storefront b-roll, money in the title · tones: cheerleader, hype
investor, grim skeptic narrator · CTAs: free consultation, "check territory availability"
(scarcity), free guide, "comment FRANCHISE" · words: financial freedom, be your own boss, proven
system, blueprint, playbook, recession-proof, empire, journey, right fit.
**Midlife-exit content:** "I got laid off at [age] after [N] years" · wound → "blessing in
disguise" / "new chapter" · faith-inflected earnestness · tearful confessional to camera · faceless
ocean b-roll with melancholy music · listicles of "companies that WANT you" · smug "should've
saved" · AI-voiced revenge fiction with cartoon justice (the audience mocks its wrong details).
**Also banned for us (compliance):** any dollar outcome, payback period or hours-per-week figure
from a character; gross revenue implied as income; top-performer anchoring; scarcity pressure.

## 3. THE SIGNAL — the audience's own words (from comments; short quotes)
- Loyalty betrayed: "If you die, your job will be posted before your obituary" (5,115 likes) ·
  "We are a resource."
- Ageism logic trap: "The people that want us to work until 70 are the same people that won't hire
  us after 50" · too experienced and not experienced enough at once · "Botoxify your resume".
- Quietly done: "sometimes I hope for a layoff" · "praying for a package" · relief when fired ·
  "When your spirit leaves, take your body with you" (3,683 likes).
- The step-down humiliation: "baking chickens in the deli" · trained by a 22-year-old who
  "speaks to me in the most condescending manner" · "$16 an hour".
- The bridge to 65: COBRA, ACA, "health care for wife and me until 65".
- The spouse: the embarrassed wife, "she stayed when the ship crashed", resentment of other people's
  safety nets.
- Distrust of buying a business: "People don't sell profitable businesses" · "the owner WAS the
  business" · hotel-seminar gurus.
- Rare but honest: "it takes me longer to learn new things now."
- Handle with extreme care: comments linking a job to suicidal thoughts. Never used for effect.

## 4. THE UNCLAIMED GROUND — where Waypoint stories can live
1. **The person hoping to be laid off** — "you're outsourcing your courage to HR."
2. **The kitchen-table negotiation** — one partner's risk is the other partner's retirement.
3. **The humiliation ladder played with wit** — VP to deli counter; the 22-year-old manager.
4. **Corporate euphemism satire** — "You weren't laid off. You were transitioned to your next
   chapter." (the one rant that did this broke out at 436×).
5. **The meeting you weren't invited to** — told from the HR person / note-taker's side.
6. **The logic trap** — work till 70, unemployable at 50.
7. **Golden handcuffs made literal** — the health-insurance bridge as the real reason people stay.
8. **Admitting you've gotten slower** — and why that's a reason not to compete with 28-year-olds
   on their turf.
9. **Arguing with the skeptic on their terms** — "most people shouldn't buy a franchise"; who
   pays the broker, said out loud (needs FranChoice compliance review).
10. **The advisor with scar tissue** — no consultant admits running a location at a loss. (A stance
    and credibility point; our ads themselves stay fiction.)
Grounding rule from the audience: fiction must get the details right (PIP, FMLA, severance weeks,
COBRA) — this audience mocks corporate stories that don't.

## 5. Craft from human-made animation (7 films watched: Chipotle "Back to the Start",
Aardman "Creature Comforts", "Dumb Ways to Die", "El Empleo", John Lewis "Bear & Hare",
Hertzfeldt "Rejected", Apple "Share Your Gifts")
What they do that AI ads almost never do:
- **One idea held the whole time** (people-as-furniture for 6 minutes; one camera move for 2).
- **Stillness** — shots held 25–40 seconds read as confidence. AI cuts every 2–4s.
- **Colour that tells the story** — one accent colour with meaning; grey *is* the plot in Chipotle.
- **The medium admitted** — miniature scale, paper grain, clay thumbprints, jittering lines.
- **Faces that do less** — peg dolls, dot eyes, a blank stare. Big expressive "Pixar eyes" is where
  polish becomes generic (Apple is the warning: closest to the AI default).
- **Sound as a second idea** — real rambling voices in clay mouths; a cheerful song over death;
  silence.
- **Brand late and inside the world** — on a lorry at 75%; product in the background.
- **Withholding** — deaths off-screen, the art kept hidden, the job revealed only at the end.
Candidate signature directions (options, untested, each with a risk): Office Diorama · Vox-Pop Clay ·
Human Furniture · Cheerful Casualties · Rejected Memo · Drawn-on-the-Real. Cross-cutting rules to
consider: at least one shot held 8s+; one meaningful accent colour; visible medium texture; brand
no earlier than ~75%, inside the world.
